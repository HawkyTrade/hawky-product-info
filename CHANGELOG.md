# Hawky product updates

This page lists confirmed, user-visible product changes. Release availability and included features can vary by platform, distribution track, account access, and configuration.

For the latest version available to you, use the official links on [hawky.trade](https://www.hawky.trade). Release notes shown by an app store or distribution platform may contain additional platform-specific details.

## October 2026 — Solana Mobile launch (1.0.1)

- Hawky Trade launched in the Solana Mobile dApp Store on October 23, 2026.
- Brought configurable signal analysis, position monitoring, and decision review to a mobile-friendly workspace.
- Paper Mode uses live market data with simulated positions; no external wallet connection is required.

## October 2026 — Android early access (1.0.4)

- Made position exposure easier to read.
- Refined push-notification presentation and overall stability.
