# Paper Mode

Paper Mode is a way to explore Hawky's workflow using live market data with simulated execution. Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or a flow for connecting an external wallet. Paper Mode does not include an external-wallet connection step.

## What Paper Mode is useful for

- Learning the interface and reviewing how configured preferences affect signal review.
- Observing market conditions without risking funds through a Hawky-executed transaction.
- Reviewing simulated activity and historical outcomes as research material.

## Important limits

Paper execution is simulated. It cannot reproduce every condition of a live transaction, including changing liquidity, network conditions, fees, execution delays, or price movement. Paper results are not a forecast and do not guarantee a similar live outcome.

Paper Mode does not remove the risks of researching or trading digital assets. Verify contract addresses and make independent decisions.
