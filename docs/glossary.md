# Hawky glossary

**Alert**  
A notification about a configured Hawky event. Available event types and delivery depend on account settings and platform support.

**CA (contract address)**  
The on-chain address that identifies a token or contract. Verify an address independently before using it.

**Entry filters**  
User-configurable criteria used to evaluate whether a signal is eligible for further review or action. Exact options depend on the account and platform.

**Live market data**  
Market information that reflects current or recent conditions. It may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable.

**Paper Mode**  
A Hawky mode that uses market data with simulated execution. Paper results do not predict real execution or future outcomes.

**Qualified signal**  
A signal that passes the entry and security checks configured for that account. It is not a guarantee of safety or performance.

**Signal source**  
A source from which Hawky can receive signals, when that source is enabled and available to the account.

**Position**  
A tracked token exposure within Hawky. Displayed values and simulated results may differ from outcomes under real market conditions.

**Push notification**  
An alert delivered through a supported browser or device after the user enables it and grants permission. Delivery depends on platform, device, browser, and network support.

**Live execution**  
Execution of real transactions where the feature is available and configured. Unlike Paper Mode, it can involve real funds and financial loss.
