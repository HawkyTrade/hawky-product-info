# Hawky glossary

Plain-language descriptions of Hawky's user-facing terms and tools. Availability can vary by platform, account access, and configuration. These descriptions explain what a feature is for; they do not document internal rules or implementation details.

**Alert**
A notice about an event in your Hawky account. Available event types and delivery depend on your settings and platform support.

**Algorithm**
The workspace for evaluating strategy behavior from available completed-position evidence and, where enabled, adapting a strategy. It does not guarantee an outcome.

**Brain**
AI-assisted signal analysis available in supported Hawky workflows. Its output is decision support, not a guarantee or prediction.

**Analytics**
Summaries of activity and results over a selected period. The information shown depends on available records and the selected mode or account.

**Buy Protection**
A configurable protection for managing position exposure and gains after a buy. Its effect depends on the selected settings; it cannot eliminate market or execution risk.

**CA (contract address)**
The on-chain address that identifies a token or contract. Check that an address is correct before using it.

**CA Alerts**
Alerts associated with a contract address you choose to follow. The available conditions and notifications depend on your account settings.

**Copy CA**
Copies a token's contract address so you can research or use it elsewhere. Copying an address does not execute a transaction.

**Custom signal source**
A signal source you choose to receive, when that source is supported and enabled for your account.

**Edge (probabilistic analysis)**
An additional probability-based view alongside your configured strategy. It is an estimate for decision support, not a prediction or guarantee.

**Entry band**
A configured range used to describe where you want a candidate to be considered for entry. The available range settings are shown in your account.

**Entry filters**
Your configurable criteria for evaluating whether a signal fits your entry preferences. Available criteria depend on your account and platform.

**Entry profile**
A saved set of entry preferences used when reviewing signals. Review the selected profile to understand which preferences are active.

**Execution**
Public Website and mobile accounts use simulated Paper execution and do not submit on-chain trades.

**External wallet connection**
Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or a flow for connecting an external wallet.

**Filtered signal**
A signal that Hawky has evaluated using the preferences configured for the account. Its status indicates the outcome of that review.

**Flat price exit**
A configurable position-exit option for conditions where price movement remains limited. Its availability and behavior depend on the selected settings.

**Real-time market data**
Market information reflecting current or recent conditions. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable.

**Market quality**
An entry-analysis area for reviewing selected market conditions. Passing these checks does not guarantee that a token is safe or that a trade will succeed.

**Momentum confirmation**
An entry-analysis option that helps assess recent movement when reviewing a candidate. It is one input to a decision, not a prediction.

**Paper Mode**
The mode available to public Website and mobile accounts. It uses available live or recent market data to record simulated positions; it does not submit on-chain trades or hold user funds.

**PNL (profit and loss)**
The recorded or estimated gain or loss for a position or period. Unrealized values can change with the market; Paper values are simulated.

**Position**
A token exposure tracked in Hawky. Displayed values may change and may differ from the result of a real transaction.

**Position exposure**
A view of the value represented by positions in the selected mode. It is an estimate and does not guarantee the amount that could be recovered by selling.

**Push notification**
An alert delivered through a supported browser or device after you enable it and grant permission. Delivery depends on platform, device, browser, and network support.

**Qualified signal**
A signal that passes the entry and security checks configured for the account. It is information to review, not a guarantee of safety or performance.

**Quote freshness**
How recent a price quote is when it is displayed or used in an evaluation. Hawky may identify a quote as stale when it is no longer sufficiently current for that use.

**Random entry**
A configurable entry option that introduces variation into the entry process. Its effect depends on the settings shown in your account.

**Reversal**
A change in recent market direction or activity that may be considered during signal analysis. It does not establish that a trend has changed or predict what happens next.

**Risk Center**
A workspace for reviewing risk-related information and controls available to your account. It supports informed decisions but does not remove risk.

**RSI**
The Relative Strength Index, a common market-momentum indicator. Where available, Hawky can use configured RSI preferences as part of entry analysis; the indicator does not predict future prices.

**Security checks**
Checks that assess selected risk signals before a candidate is treated as qualified. Passing them is not a guarantee that a token, contract, or transaction is safe.

**Signal**
An item received from a configured source for review by Hawky. A signal is not, by itself, an instruction or guarantee to trade.

**Signal source**
A configured source from which Hawky can receive signals, when enabled and available to the account.

**Stop-loss (SL)**
A configured condition intended to limit or manage downside on a position. Fast market moves and execution conditions can affect the result.

**Stop-loss type**
The stop-loss behavior selected in the execution settings. Available types and their effects are shown in the account configuration.

**Strategy Lab**
A workspace for reviewing and comparing available strategy information. Results depend on the records and settings included in the comparison.

**Strategy Library**
A place to save and review strategy configurations. Sharing or export options may depend on the account and platform.

**Strategy Ranking**
A comparison of available strategy results. Historical results and sample sizes do not guarantee future performance.

**Take-profit (TP)**
A configured condition for taking some or all of a position's gains. The actual result can differ from the target because market and execution conditions change.

**Trailing protection**
A position-management option that adjusts protection as conditions change, according to the selected settings. It cannot guarantee an exit price.

**Tracked wallet**
A public wallet address selected for monitoring. Hawky can associate received signals with tracked addresses; this does not amount to a complete record of a wallet's activity or profitability.

**Wallet**
A user-controlled tool for holding or signing digital-asset transactions. Public Website and mobile Hawky accounts do not connect to an external wallet; optional menu payments are signed separately by the user.

**Wallet Behavior Drift**
A review of Hawky's recorded outcomes associated with signals from tracked wallets. It does not report the wallet's complete on-chain history or claim the wallet itself earned those results.

**Watchlist**
A list for observing selected tokens or addresses and reviewing related information. A watchlist entry does not trigger a transaction by itself.

**Work Offline**
Where supported, lets a configured Hawky session continue while you are away. Push delivery still depends on your notification settings, device, and platform support.
