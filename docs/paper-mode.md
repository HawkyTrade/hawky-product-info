# Paper Mode: simulated trading, no wallet connection or funds required

Public Website and mobile accounts currently use Paper Mode only. It lets you explore Hawky with available live or recent market data and simulated execution. Every position recorded in Paper Mode is virtual: it does not submit an on-chain trade, require connecting an external wallet, or hold or move real funds. Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or another external-wallet connection feature. Official Hawky account sign-in and Paper Mode flows never ask for a recovery phrase or private key.

## What Paper Mode is useful for

- Learning the interface and reviewing how configured preferences affect signal analysis.
- Observing market conditions without submitting a real transaction through Paper Mode.
- Reviewing simulated activity and position outcomes as research material.

## Signals and other platforms

Signals are information to review, not instructions or guarantees. If you copy a contract address (CA) to research or use elsewhere, verify it independently and make your own decision. Hawky is not affiliated with third-party platforms you may choose to use.

## Protect yourself from impersonators

For help recognizing impersonators and finding official links, see [Responsible use and security](responsible-use.md). Never share a recovery phrase or private key with anyone.

## Important limits

Paper execution is simulated. It cannot reproduce every condition of a real transaction, including changing liquidity, network conditions, fees, execution delays, or price movement. Paper results are not a forecast and do not guarantee future outcomes.

Paper Mode does not remove the risks of researching or trading digital assets. Verify contract addresses and make independent decisions.
