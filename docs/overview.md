# Hawky Trade

Hawky Trade is a configurable workspace for reviewing Solana signals, current market context, and position outcomes. It brings signal discovery, configurable analysis, alerts, position management, and strategy review into one user-controlled workflow.

## What users can do

### Receive and review signals

Users can work with supported built-in and custom signal sources, including tracked-wallet signals where available. Hawky applies the entry preferences and security checks configured for the account and presents the resulting signal for review. A qualified signal reflects those configured checks at the time of evaluation; it is not a guarantee of token safety, execution, or profit.

Users can review a signal in Hawky, copy its contract address (CA) for independent research, or decide how to use it in another platform. Copying a CA does not initiate a trade.

### Configure analysis

Available entry preferences can include entry ranges, market-quality checks, momentum confirmation, reversal context, and RSI. Random Entry is available as an optional way to add variation to the configured entry process. Security checks provide additional risk information before a candidate is treated as qualified. The controls shown in each account determine which options are available and active.

Brain provides AI-assisted analysis where enabled. Algorithm helps users review strategy behavior from recorded outcomes and can adapt a strategy where that capability is enabled. Edge adds a probability-based perspective alongside the user's own rules. These tools support review and decision-making; they do not predict or guarantee market outcomes.

### Use Paper Mode or supported execution

Paper Mode uses current or recent market information with simulated execution. It can be used to explore signal analysis, position management, and recorded outcomes without submitting on-chain trades. Paper results are simulated and do not predict Live execution or future performance.

Where a platform and account support Live execution, users can explicitly configure Hawky to submit real on-chain transactions. Live execution involves real funds and can result in financial loss. Available modes and controls vary by platform, account access, and configuration.

### Manage and review positions

Position tools can include take-profit targets, stop-loss options, trailing protection, Buy Protection, and Flat Price Exit. Users can review position charts, available quote information, exit reasons, and portfolio or activity records. Market movement, liquidity, quote freshness, network conditions, and execution can cause actual results to differ from configured targets or displayed estimates.

Portfolio and Wallets views organize the account's available balances and positions; Activity provides a record of supported account actions. Depending on the platform and records available, performance views can summarize PNL, trade activity, and historical results across selected periods. Paper figures represent simulated activity.

Risk Center presents account exposure and other risk-related information where available. Strategy Lab helps users compare saved strategy profiles; Strategy Library supports saving, reviewing, and exchanging strategy information; Strategy Ranking compares available strategy results. Users can also share a closed-position result card or create and verify a Signal Passport for an eligible Hawky decision. Historical records and comparisons are limited to the data and periods shown; past results do not guarantee future outcomes.

Wallet Behavior Drift reviews Hawky outcomes associated with signals from tracked wallets. It is not a complete record of a wallet's on-chain activity and does not claim that the tracked wallet earned Hawky's displayed results.

### Set alerts and continue a session

CA Alerts let users follow selected contract addresses and supported conditions. Users can choose which supported event notifications they want and enable delivery through a compatible browser or device. Delivery depends on account settings, platform support, permissions, connectivity, and device settings.

Where supported, Work Offline lets an enabled session continue for a configured period while the user is away from the interface. It does not guarantee uninterrupted operation or notification delivery.

Setup Guide provides a guided introduction to the workspace and its available account setup steps.

## Wallet connection

Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or a flow for connecting an existing external wallet. Paper Mode does not require a wallet connection. Any wallet-management or execution features shown in a supported platform are separate, account-dependent product capabilities.

## A user-controlled workflow

Configure the available signal sources and analysis preferences, review the information Hawky presents, then choose whether to observe, use Paper Mode, copy a CA for independent research, or use another explicitly available action. Hawky provides tools and recorded context; the user remains in control of their choices.

## Availability and limitations

Features can vary by platform, account access, selected mode, and configuration. Market data, signals, quotes, alerts, and network services can be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable. Hawky's analysis and statistics are decision-support information, not a guarantee that a token is safe, an order will complete, or a strategy will be profitable.
