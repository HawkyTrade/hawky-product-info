# Responsible use and security

Hawky Trade is a software workspace for market-signal review and configurable trading workflows. It does not guarantee profits and is not financial, investment, or legal advice.

Digital assets can be highly volatile. A signal, alert, score, chart, or past result may be incomplete or become stale as market conditions change. Verify information independently before acting.

Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or any flow for connecting an external wallet. If a site or app claiming to be Hawky asks you to connect an external wallet or approve a wallet-connection request, close it and reach Hawky through [hawky.trade](https://www.hawky.trade). Hawky will never ask you to enter a wallet recovery phrase or private key into a website. Never share those secrets with anyone.

Historical and simulated results do not predict future outcomes. Users are responsible for their own decisions and for complying with applicable laws and platform terms.
