# Responsible use and security

Hawky Trade is a software workspace for market-signal review and configurable trading workflows. It does not guarantee profits and is not financial, investment, or legal advice.

Digital assets can be highly volatile. A signal, alert, score, chart, or past result may be incomplete or become stale as market conditions change. Verify information independently before acting.

Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or any flow for connecting an external wallet. Paper Mode uses simulated positions and does not submit on-chain trades, require an external-wallet connection, or hold or move real funds. Official account sign-in and Paper Mode flows never ask for a recovery phrase or private key. If a site, message, or app claiming to be Hawky asks you to connect an external wallet or share a recovery phrase, it is not an official Hawky flow. Close it and reach Hawky through [hawky.trade](https://www.hawky.trade). Never share a recovery phrase or private key with anyone.

Signals and contract addresses copied from Hawky are for research. If you choose to use another platform, verify the information and platform independently; Hawky is not affiliated with third-party platforms. Historical and simulated results do not predict future outcomes. Users are responsible for their own decisions and for complying with applicable laws and platform terms.
