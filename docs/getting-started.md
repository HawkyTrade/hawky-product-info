# Getting started with Hawky Trade

This guide introduces the basic Hawky workflow. Features and access can vary by platform, account, and configuration.

## 1. Open an official Hawky app

Use [hawky.trade](https://www.hawky.trade) or the official app link published on the website. On Android, Hawky Trade is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=trade.hawky.app).

## 2. Create and activate your account

Create an account with your username and email address. Follow the password setup link sent to your email, then return to Hawky and sign in.

## 3. Review your workspace

Use the in-product setup guidance to review the settings and features available to your account. Hawky does not offer WalletConnect or a flow for connecting an external wallet.

## 4. Start with Paper Mode

Select **Paper Mode** before starting a Hawky session. Paper Mode uses live market data with simulated execution. It is intended for exploring the workflow and reviewing how your configured preferences affect signal evaluation.

Before starting, review the signal sources and settings available to your account. Their availability may differ by platform or account access.

## 5. Review signals and alerts

A qualified-signal alert indicates that a signal passed the configured entry and security checks for that account. Review the information in Hawky before deciding what to do. Where available, you can copy a contract address (CA) for independent research or use elsewhere; copying it does not execute a trade on another platform.

To receive push alerts, open **Alerts** and choose the notification types you want. Grant the browser or device permission if prompted. Delivery depends on the platform, device settings, background support, and network connection, so do not rely on push alerts as your only source for time-sensitive information.

## 6. Review results carefully

Paper results are simulated and cannot reproduce every real transaction condition. They do not predict or guarantee future outcomes. See [Paper Mode](paper-mode.md) and [Responsible use and security](responsible-use.md) for more information.
